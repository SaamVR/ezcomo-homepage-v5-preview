FAQ

Before you start.

Can I start without coding?

You can start from a template and edit your storefront visually. Payment, delivery and domain connections may require additional setup.

Can I use EZComo alongside Facebook or Instagram?

Yes. Share your storefront link from your social channels so customers can browse products and place orders in one place.

Which payment options can I offer?

EZComo supports local checkout options including bKash, Nagad and cash on delivery. Automated payment confirmation depends on the method and your account setup.

Can I book a courier from an order?

The supported Pathao workflow can prepare bookings when your merchant connection is configured. Check the requirements for your chosen courier before relying on automation.

Can I use my own domain?

Eligible plans support a custom domain after DNS setup and verification.

What happens after I choose a template?

Your selected starting point is carried into signup. You can then add your brand and content using the tools included in your plan.

Are the stores and orders on this page real?

The examples use sample businesses and sample data. The homepage walkthrough does not take payments, place real orders or book a courier.

How do I get help?

Visit EZComo support for the current contact options and setup help.
